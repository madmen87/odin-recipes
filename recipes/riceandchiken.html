Chicken and rice cooked together in one pot is seriously good. The rice absorbs all the juices from the chicken and any vegetables you use, plus broth flavourings. The flavour combinations are endless. Today, we’re doing Nando’s Portuguese-style chicken and rice, complete with a drizzle of Peri-naise. So good!

Ingredients

  • 1 whole chicken, cut into pieces
  • 2 cups of rice
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves of garlic, minced
  • 1 bell pepper, chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 2 cups of chicken broth
  • 1 teaspoon of paprika
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Season the chicken with salt, pepper, and paprika.
  2. In a large pot, heat some oil and brown the chicken pieces on all sides. Remove the chicken and set aside.
  3. In the same pot, sauté the onion, garlic, and bell pepper until softened.
  4. Add the chopped tomatoes and cook for a few minutes until they break down.
  5. Add the rice to the pot and stir to coat it with the vegetables and spices.
  6. Pour in the chicken broth and bring to a boil.
  7. Return the chicken to the pot, cover, and reduce heat to low. Simmer for about 20-25 minutes, or until the rice is cooked and the chicken is tender.
  8. Remove from heat and let it sit for a few minutes before serving. Enjoy!
